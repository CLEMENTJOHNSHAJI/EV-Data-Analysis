Cumulative EV Fleet — Annual Growth Rate

Helsinki, Tampere & Oulu · Source: Traficom (kanta_010) · Passenger cars in traffic by year of first registration

Annual Growth Rate = (Cumulative EV Fleetyear − Cumulative EV Fleetyear-1) / Cumulative EV Fleetyear-1 × 100. Cumulative fleet = running sum of annual EV additions (BEV + Petrol PHEV + Diesel PHEV) from 2010.

The chart starts at 2013. Growth rates for 2011–2012 are omitted from the trend because the fleet was near zero then (e.g. Tampere/Oulu had 0–4 vehicles), which makes the percentage change explode (hundreds to thousands of %) and not comparable. Those values are still listed in the table below.
